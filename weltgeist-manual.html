01 / Setup

In the Pedal Chain

Place Weltgeist before your compressor so it captures the full difference between soft and hard picking before the compressor evens out the levels.

Audio Signal

Guitar→ Weltgeist→ Compressor

From the compressor, run the audio into your effect pedal and then to your amp.

Expression Control

Weltgeist EXP→ Effect EXP IN

Use a TRS cable to connect a Weltgeist EXP output to the expression input of a compatible effect pedal. On the effect pedal, select the parameter you want to control.

02 / Controls

The Controls

Sensitivity
Higher Sensitivity gives you more expression movement from the same picking strength.
Rise
A short Rise follows the attack quickly. A long Rise makes the expression position change more gradually as you play louder.
Fall
Sets how quickly the expression position returns to its resting point as the signal fades.
Base
Sets the resting expression position when the input is quiet.
Invert
Reverses the direction of expression movement.

Set Base while playing softly. Adjust Sensitivity with hard attacks, then fine-tune Rise and Fall.

Rise and Fall affect expression travel. A slow Rise can keep short notes from reaching full travel, even at maximum Sensitivity. A fast Fall can reset the position between notes.

03 / Examples

Applications

Dynamic Modulation

Dig in to speed up Tremolo or deepen Chorus, Flanger, Vibrato or Phaser on accents.

Auto-Swell

Use a slow Rise and a low Base to fade notes in through an expression-controllable Volume Knob.

Dynamic Dirt

Control the clean/dirty blend to bring in more Overdrive, Fuzz or Distortion as you pick harder.

Envelope Filter & Dynamic EQ

Open a Filter with a hard attack or sweep the center frequency of an EQ band with your picking dynamics.

Dynamic Delay & Reverb

Push Delay Feedback with hard attacks. For Ducking, reverse the mix response so delay repeats or reverb tails come forward between phrases.

Sidechain Ducking

Feed the bass or bassdrum into Weltgeist. Use EXP to turn down the guitar’s Volume or cut just its low end with an EQ while the bass plays.

7. Live audio input

The Weltgeist simulation provides an optional Live Audio mode. When you actively select this function, your browser requests permission to access an available audio input device, such as a microphone or audio interface.

The incoming audio signal is processed locally in your browser using browser audio functionality. The simulation analyses the signal level in order to control the simulated expression response.

The audio signal itself is not transmitted by the Weltgeist simulation to Late Roman Audio or to an external server, and Late Roman Audio does not record or store the audio input.

You can stop the use of the audio input by switching the simulation back to Manual mode. You can also revoke access to the audio input through your browser's site-permission settings.
